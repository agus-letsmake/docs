---
title: Evidence
description: "Attachments and observed step screenshots for a result"
---

**Evidence** is the reviewable artifact trail for a case result: file attachments
and observed step screenshots (walkthrough).

With `TESTLAB_PUSH=1`, the Client enables evidence by default. Opt out with
`TESTLAB_EVIDENCE=0`. Modes: `failures` (default), `critical`, or `all`.

HTTP surfaces (Observed):

- `POST /api/attachments/upload` — result-level attachments
- `POST /api/evidence/v1/ingest` — case attempt / observed steps
- `POST /api/evidence/v1/steps/:stepId/artifacts` — step screenshots

See the [Client package](/docs/cli-sdk/client) for helpers.
